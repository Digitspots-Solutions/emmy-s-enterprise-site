Who We Are

High Fliers Cross-Border Payments Limited (also known as High Fliers International Remittance Co) is a leading Nigerian fintech company specializing in secure, efficient cross-border payment solutions. Based in Maiduguri, Borno State, we serve clients across Nigeria and internationally, providing seamless remittance services that connect families, businesses, and communities worldwide.

Our Mission

To democratize international payments by making them accessible, affordable, and lightning-fast for every Nigerian, regardless of location.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted cross-border payment platform, bridging the gap between Nigeria and the global economy.

Send Money in 3 Simple Steps

1

Create Your Account

Register online or visit our Maiduguri office. Provide your valid ID (NIN, Driver's License, or International Passport) and complete our quick verification process. Account setup takes just minutes.

2

Choose Your Destination & Amount

Select the recipient country, enter the amount you wish to send, and see the exact amount your beneficiary will receive. Our transparent pricing means no hidden fees—what you see is what you pay.

3

Send & Track

Fund your transaction via bank transfer, card payment, or cash deposit at our office. Your money is on its way! Track your transfer in real-time and receive confirmation when your beneficiary collects their funds.

First-Time Sender?

Enjoy special rates on your first transaction. Our customer support team will guide you through every step to ensure a smooth experience.

Required Documents:

Valid government-issued ID • Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement) • Beneficiary details (name, account number, bank details)
